# Code of Conduct

We aim to make participation in ZIP-to-Workspace welcoming and respectful.

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share relevant technical context without exposing private data.
- Do not harass, threaten, or discriminate against contributors.
- Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation when necessary.

Report serious conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
